Frequently Asked Questions

What industries does Infix Media specialize in?

We work with D2C brands, luxury tech firms, financial portals, real estate companies, and premium consumer goods. If your brand wants to establish a premium and dominant presence in the digital landscape, we have customized blueprints ready.

How long does a premium website project take?

A typical corporate landing page or a highly aesthetic marketing site takes about 2 to 4 weeks, including UX wireframes, visual curation, custom interactions, responsiveness tuning, and testing. Complex web platforms with database backends can range from 4 to 8 weeks.

Do you manage ad spend budgets directly?

Clients retain direct ownership of their ad accounts (Meta, Google) and are billed directly by those platforms. Infix Media charges a flat management fee or a performance percentage based on the scope, ensuring there are no conflicts of interest.

What does "Creative First" mean for my ROI?

Standard static ads are ignored in modern social feeds. "Creative First" means we design assets that physically capture eyes and arrest thumb movement. Highly optimized visual hooks directly increase CTR (Click-Through Rate), lowering click costs and substantially improving your final ROI.

How do we begin working together?

Simply complete the Contact Form below with details about your brand, service requirements, and budget framework. Our strategy coordinators will review your input and schedule a free consultation session within 24 hours.
